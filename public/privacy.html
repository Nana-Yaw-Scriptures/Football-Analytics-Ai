Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Scorina AI is a football analytics platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — When you sign up, we collect your email address and name via Google OAuth (Supabase Auth).
  • Usage data — Pages visited, features used, and prediction history within the app.
  • Prediction data — Match predictions and picks you submit are stored to calculate your accuracy and leaderboard ranking.
  • Device information — Device type, operating system, and app version for debugging purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Scorina AI service
  • To display your prediction history and leaderboard ranking
  • To authenticate your account securely
  • To send important service notifications (not marketing)
  • To fix bugs and improve app performance

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider with enterprise-grade security. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Scorina AI uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Authentication and database (privacy policy)
  • API-Football — Live football data (no personal data shared)
  • Understat — Football statistics (no personal data shared)
  • Google OAuth — Sign-in service (privacy policy)

5. Analytics Disclaimer

Scorina AI provides football analytics and AI-generated predictions for entertainment purposes only. We do not facilitate gambling. All predictions are probabilistic and not guarantees. Users are responsible for complying with local laws.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your prediction history

8. Children's Privacy

Scorina AI is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure authentication, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of Scorina AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at:
